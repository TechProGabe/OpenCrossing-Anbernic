The NES games in your house work now, town runs a lot smoother, and the whole thing feels more like part of the game.

**Dia2809 is now officially a maintainer.** Dia's fork is where this project started, so it's great to have them on board for real.

**What's new**
- **NES games work.** Play the NES furniture in your house, keep your high scores, and hold L1 + R1 + R2 to go back to your room. You can also put your own `.nes` files in `ports/ac-gc/nes_roms/` and play them on the plain NES item.
- **Better FPS in town.** The game was updating and drawing every character and object nearby, even ones off screen. It doesn't anymore, and town now sits close to 60 fps.
- **Endless acres.** The camera follows you smoothly from acre to acre instead of stopping at every edge. On by default.
- **A new Options page** that looks like it belongs in the game. Open it from the title screen, or press Select while playing.
- **A new loading screen**, and an **Exit to OS** option on the title screen.
- **Bells to wallet.** Bell bags you pick up go straight into your wallet. On by default.
- The D-pad now works in menus and speeds up text.

**Fixes**
- Diary furniture and a few other items showed the wrong colors, like hot pink.
- Nook sometimes didn't show up, which could softlock the game.
- No more black screen at the mailbox after visiting another town, and no more missing sounds after a train ride.
- Quitting without saving right after a trip no longer throws the trip away.
- Fixes for the weather after a restart, the island house door, the radio, and a batch of smaller crashes from the decomp.
- No more freezes of a few seconds with logging turned on, and less wasted work every frame.
- Patched or translated discs now show their own menu labels.
- If the game crashes, it writes `crash.log` next to `log.txt`. Please attach both when you report a bug.

**Upgrading**
Back up `ports/ac-gc/save`, then copy the new files over the old ones. Your settings carry over.
